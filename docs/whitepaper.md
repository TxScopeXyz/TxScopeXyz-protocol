# TxScope Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Fast-moving markets hide their best signals in plain sight: a pool that suddenly doubles its turnover, a pair whose depth thins out overnight. TxScope exists to make those moments easy to see.

Our mission is to give anyone on Robinhood Chain a clear, current view of where activity is gathering, using public data, with every figure traceable to its source and nothing invented.

## Principles

1. **Source before story.** A number appears only when a source states it. Missing figures render as a dash or the word pending.
2. **No forecasts.** TxScope reports what has traded, not what might.
3. **No custody, no prompts.** The site never asks for a wallet connection or a signature.
4. **Plain failure.** When a feed breaks the page says so and keeps the last good data on screen.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Lens.** Live trending-pool view, ticker, re-sorting, contract slot, public launch of the site.

**Phase 2: Depth.** Per-pool detail pages with history, side-by-side comparison of two pools, shareable links.

**Phase 3: Signals.** Configurable move and turnover alerts delivered without accounts or custody.

**Phase 4: Open data.** A documented read-only feed so other builders can reuse what TxScope has indexed.
