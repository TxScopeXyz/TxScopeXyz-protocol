Method

From noise to signal in three moves

A pool that suddenly draws volume tells you something before the headline does. TxScope watches the index for those moments, ranks what it finds, and lets you re-order the list to suit the question you are asking.

  1. 1

    Locate

    At load, the page asks the index which network id belongs to Robinhood Chain. Nothing is wired in by hand.

  2. 2

    Rank

    The index returns its trending pools. The first ten are shown, and you can re-sort them by volume, depth or momentum.

  3. 3

    Renew

    Numbers refresh every minute and rest while the tab is out of sight. A failed refresh never wipes what you already see.
